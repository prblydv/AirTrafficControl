# Delhi airport geometry and imagery

Updated 19 November 2026.

**Current rendering supersedes the historical MapKit description below:** The game now bundles the user's 1672×941 Expanded Delhi Airport Satellite Terrain.png unchanged. It has no live map or network dependency. AirportLayout.swift maps simulation data into this edited artwork; aircraft are enlarged and apron stands spaced for gameplay. Source geometry and edited photographic features are not guaranteed to be survey-identical. The prior generated edit/prompt and registration metadata are kept under art/.

The app displays real satellite imagery using Apple's native MapKit imagery map style. Images are supplied at runtime by Apple Maps; their date, coverage and resolution can vary by zoom. Apple attribution remains native. No Google Maps screenshots or downloaded satellite tiles are bundled. A real MapKit satellite snapshot was loaded and inspected during implementation, without adding that photograph to the distributed app.

`VIDP-runways.json` is the Delhi subset of OurAirports runway data, retrieved from https://davidmegginson.github.io/ourairports-data/runways.csv. Runway designations were cross-checked with the airport operator: https://www.newdelhiairport.in/factsheet. OurAirports data is public domain: https://ourairports.com/data/.

`VIDP-osm.json` preserves airport features extracted from OpenStreetMap API bounding-box queries over longitude 77.06–77.14 and latitude 28.53–28.59, retrieved 19 November 2026. It contains 644 taxiway ways, 10 terminal footprints, runway ways, aprons and tagged holding/parking positions. Source geometry is © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under ODbL: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/. Attribution is visible on the satellite view; the source subset is kept here for inspection and reuse.

`scripts/build_delhi_ground.py` reconstructs the connected taxiway graph, selects tagged holding points at the western ends of all four runways and mapped runway-exit intersections, and computes 40 paths: 12 departures, 12 arrivals, 12 inter-runway reroutes and 4 runway entries. Paths favor avoiding runway crossings. Stand positions are mapped Bay 41, Bay 42 and Bay 43; each has an approximately 54–60 projected metre apron connection to its nearest taxi centerline. These short connections are computed projections, not surveyed stand lead-in paint. The generated `AirTrafficControl/DelhiGroundData.swift` embeds the paths and terminal footprints, so iOS, macOS and simulation checks use exactly the same dataset. Regenerate with `python3 scripts/build_delhi_ground.py`.

Coordinates are WGS84 latitude/longitude. `DelhiLayout` converts to spherical Mercator with radius 6378137 m and a fixed origin (77.045° E, 28.59° N). One simulation unit equals ten projected metres on both axes. The inverse projection supplies every MapKit annotation/route coordinate. This avoids stretching either axis independently. Runway headings and aircraft rotations use that same map plane. The map is north-up and flat; rotation and pitch gestures are disabled. Independent checks compare the projection against MKMapPoint and verify sub-nanodegree numeric round trips.

Mathematical alignment does not improve the underlying source survey accuracy. Satellite capture dates and OpenStreetMap/OurAirports updates can differ. Terminal 1 and 3 labels are located on their mapped footprints; the Terminal 2 label identifies its central building. Aircraft symbols are deliberately enlarged for selection. Paint visible in the satellite layer is photographic; optional labels identify threshold numbers without claiming to reproduce each painted character as an editable object.

Gameplay remains deliberately simplified: taxi route selection is precomputed and bidirectional, crossings conservatively reserve the relevant runway for the taxi movement, and the shared ground corridor allows one taxiing aircraft at a time. Runway closures prevent new conflicting clearances. This is not an operational aviation chart or a full simulation of real Delhi procedures.

The generated dataset also embeds all 644 mapped taxiway ways for Sharp map. This is a vector rendering, not a deblurred photograph. Incoming flight positions are constructed on spherical great-circle bearings at 12/16/20 NM from runway thresholds; numeric distance checks verify those ranges. Aircraft then use simplified game arrival fixes and continuous holding loops. These routes are not real published procedures.
